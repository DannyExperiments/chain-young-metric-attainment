# Claim scope and limitations

- The result concerns equal-length rational step words identified up to equality almost everywhere. Chains use rational move intervals at fixed length, as in Messick's Definition 3.7.
- The run-transport formula holds with arbitrary metric-alphabet distances. Rational distances between each source letter and each target letter suffice for a finite chain to attain the infimum, using only the endpoint letters.
- The irrational-distance example proves nonattainment for a specific family under rational moves. The paper excludes changing-length chains and real endpoints for individual moves from its scope.
- The quadratic-form transport framework, the grid refinement and the rational minimizer fact have separate sources. The manuscript makes no priority claim.
- The finite replay corroborates the fixed-grid comparison and the analytical example. The proof of the general result is mathematical.

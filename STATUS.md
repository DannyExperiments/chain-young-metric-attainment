# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/chain-young-metric-attainment/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24568149)

Messick's fixed-length chain Young metric on rational step words has a finite minimizing chain when distances between source and target letters are rational. The chain can use only the letters of the two words. The cost is a quadratic run-transport minimum with a strict inversion term. The result uses rational move intervals and words identified up to almost-everywhere equality. A two-letter example gives nonattainment for irrational letter distances strictly between zero and one half. The proof credits Messick's grid refinement, quadratic-form optimal transport, and classical rational quadratic minimizers.

Internal mathematical review covered the statement and the proof. External peer review and formal verification have not been conducted. The exact replay compares fixed-grid chains with run transport on 1,243 word pairs and verifies the three-move example of cost 2/9.

The README links repository verification, exact replay and PDF build workflows. See the [literature and source review](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE.md).
